Decisions applied

All 14 answers from the proposal review. Owner picks are marked (owner); the rest took the recommendation.

  1. 1Personality: C, in between (recommended)
  2. 2Brand and status colors: teal #0F766E; success #15803D, warning #B45309, danger #B91C1C (recommended)
  3. 3Light and dark: follow the system (recommended)
  4. 4Typeface: B, Plus Jakarta Sans (owner); self-hosted in the build, not loaded here
  5. 5Radius and density: C, balanced: cards 12, inputs/buttons 8, pills 999 (recommended)
  6. 6Bottom navigation: B, 5 tabs with Post in the middle, shown as "+" only; bell top right (owner)
  7. 7Money display: "250 pts · ₱250" always (recommended)
  8. 8Trust signals: B, chips on cards and profiles; tier and standing only on your own Me (owner)
  9. 9Home default: C, list with a small map strip, approximate areas only (owner)
  10. 10.1App icon: B, roof carried by two neighbors, maskable (owner)
  11. 10.2Splash and theme color: teal splash; status bar follows the app surface (recommended)
  12. 10.3Install prompt: after the first success, plus a row in Me (recommended)
  13. 10.4Push permission: B, only from Notifications in Me; no prompt after posting or applying (owner)
  14. 11Card without a photo: category icon tile (recommended)

Shown when + is tapped and /me says can_post: false, before any form is filled. If a post is refused anyway, the 409 errors.reason (dormant_review or reverify_required) shows the same screen.

API gaps and open questions

Nothing here is faked in the mockups.

  1. Retry time on 429. The body is {"message":"Too many requests.","code":"TOO_MANY_REQUESTS","errors":{}} with no time. The countdown needs Retry-After on every 429 and, in the browser, Access-Control-Expose-Headers: Retry-After; or errors.retry_after in the body.
  2. Web push. Turning push on from Notifications needs a web-push subscription endpoint; POST /api/v1/me/devices is FCM-only (also in decision 0001).
  3. Unread count for the bell. unread_count comes only with GET /api/v1/me/notifications. Fine if that call is cheap; otherwise add it to /me.
  4. Category icons. category.icon holds Material names (e.g. local_car_wash); shadcn and KokonutUI use Lucide. The app needs a mapping table, or the API sends a library-neutral key.
  5. Standing on the applicant list (plan conflict). BUILD_PLAN U4 says the applicant list shows standing; spec §6 and the API keep standing private (own /me only). This foundation follows the spec. Which is right?
