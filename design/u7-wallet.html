Wallet

The Wallet tab: balances (Available, Held for tasks, Cashing out), the history with type filters, adding points through the payment page (the API’s test page for now), the wait for the payment to be confirmed, cashing out to a GCash or Maya number, its status, and payout accounts. Built from the owner’s answers on the U7 proposal with U0–U6 tokens and components. Every frame lists its endpoint. Fake Dumaguete data: Ana R. (Piapi, profile complete) adds 150 pts on Wed 25 Nov, 9:13 AM to post the 400 pts gutter task; Ben S. (ID verified) cashes out 1,000 pts on Thu 26 Nov, 2:31 PM after being paid 380 pts. Payout numbers appear only masked, as the API returns them; no legal name is shown.

5

Payment page and waiting

The POST returns checkout_url, which opens in a new window from the tap (decision 5 A). The app goes back to Wallet with a pending card (owner’s choice, decision 6 B): it says the balance doesn’t include it yet and warns against paying twice; tapping it opens a sheet with the amount, what GCash charged, the expiry and “Open the payment page again”. GET /wallet/cash-ins/{ulid} is polled every 3 s for 2 minutes, then every 15 s, and when the app becomes visible, until paid, failed or expired. The ulid is kept on the phone (no list endpoint, gap 2). Points come only from the provider’s confirmation, never from coming back.

6

Cash-in result

A toast on Wallet when the points arrive (owner’s choice, decision 7 B); from U3 the toast carries “Back to your task”. Failed, cancelled and expired don’t vanish with a toast: they stay as a card on Wallet with “Try again” (new Idempotency-Key) until dismissed.

7

Cash out

Amount, account, the ₱15 fee and what GCash receives, then a confirm sheet whose backdrop covers the dock (decision 9 A). The Idempotency-Key is made at the first “Cash out 1,000 pts” tap and kept with the body until a definite answer; a change of amount or account makes a new one (decision 12 A). API gaps (new, beyond decisions 0002–0008)

Backend work; the mockup draws around them. Still open and reused here: a readable 429 retry time (0002). Proposal gap 9 (push when payments go live) is dropped: it applied only to option 2B.

  1. 1. No fee quote before paying. The provider’s charge is known only after POST /wallet/cash-ins (amount_charged_centavos), so Add points can’t show the total before the commit. Suggest GET /wallet/cash-ins/quote?points=&channel= or the fee rate per channel on GET /wallet.
  2. 2. No list of my cash-ins (or cash-outs). The pending card relies on the cash-in ulid stored on this phone; on another phone, or after clearing data, an unpaid payment is invisible while still counting toward the 3 open payments. Suggest GET /wallet/cash-ins?status=pending (and GET /wallet/cash-outs), or pending_cash_ins on GET /wallet.
  3. 3. No failure or refund times on a cash-out. GET /wallet/cash-outs/{ulid} has paid_at and created_at only, so “comes back by …” and “refunded on …” can’t be dated. Suggest failed_at, refund_by, refunded_at.
  4. 4. History rows only carry {ulid} for cash-ins and cash-outs. Rows show “GCash •••• 4567” only with one extra call per row. Suggest embedding channel, status, account_number_masked and net_amount.
  5. 5. Failure reasons aren’t documented. Cash-in failure_reason is declined, cancelled, expired, provider_unavailable or provider_error (from the code); cash-out is any provider code or payout_failed. Suggest a documented enum each, with a fallback.
  6. 6. No history filter (owner chose filters). The phone filters loaded pages and keeps paging, so a filter with few matches can need many requests, and its empty state can only say “none in the last N” while older pages exist. Suggest GET /wallet/history?kind= (or groups: tasks, added, cash_outs); the app switches to it when available.
  7. 7. cash_in.blocked_reason = limit_reached has two causes. Daily maximum and 3 open payments give the same reason, so Wallet can’t say which before the POST. Suggest separate reasons or an open_payments count.
  8. 8. Corrections have no reason for the user. An adjustment row can’t say why. Suggest a short user-facing note (staff notes stay private).
