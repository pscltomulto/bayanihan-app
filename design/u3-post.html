Post a task

From the + tab to a live task: three steps, a review that shows the fee before anything is held, safe retries on a weak signal, and the success screen. Built from the owner’s answers on the U3 proposal, using only U0–U2 tokens and components. Every screen lists its endpoints. Fake Dumaguete data; the exact location appears only on the poster’s own screens.

1

Entering the post flow

From the + tab, the desktop rail +, or "Post a task" on a quiet-day Browse. GET /me runs first; when can_post is false the U0 screen for post_blocked_reason shows before any form. A tier-1 account stamped for re-verification still posts, so it gets no screen. A saved draft opens the resume sheet (decision 10).

2

Step 1 · What

Category (the 9 spec categories as U0 pills), title 5–60, description 20–1,000 with counters, up to 4 optional photos. Nothing is sent until Post; errors from Post that belong here bring the poster back to this step with the fields marked.

3

Step 2 · Where (decision 3: your location)

The poster stands at the job; the phone’s location becomes lat/lng with location_source: gps. The browser’s accuracy is shown in metres; over 100 m counts as poor. There is no map to drag. The location is asked for only here. Landmark and house are optional and private, like the location itself.

4

Step 3 · When and points (decision 4: pickers)

Date and time pickers limited to 1 hour to 30 days from now, matching deadline_at. The offer asks GET /tasks/quote about 400 ms after typing stops; the U0 fee box shows only what the quote returns. Range and balance come from the quote (max_points, available).

5

Add points hand-off

"Add N pts" opens Wallet’s add-points (U7) with the shortfall filled in, then returns to step 3. When GET /wallet says cash-in isn’t allowed, the hand-off explains and offers to lower the offer.

6

Review and posting

Every answer with Edit, the exact location marked "Only you", the U0 fee box, the balance after posting, and a button that names what is held. The Idempotency-Key is made when this screen opens and kept with the draft; any change makes a new one.

7

After posting

The U0 success screen (decision 9). Photos go up one by one after the 201. No push prompt (0002 #10.4). The install sheet shows once, after the first post (0002 #10.3).

8

Drafts (decision 10)

One draft on this phone, saved as you type, with its photos and its Idempotency-Key. Cleared after a successful post, on discard and on sign-out. It never posts by itself.

9

Light and dark

The page follows the phone’s setting; these frames are forced dark.

10

Widths

430 px phone, 768 px with the form column at 600 px, and desktop with the rail, the form card and a live summary.

API gaps

New in U3; backend work. Still open from 0002–0004 and used here: category list, barangay list, a readable 429 retry time.

  1. 1. The open-task limit isn’t visible before the form.can_post / post_blocked_reason don’t cover 409 LIMIT_REACHED (max_open_tasks_per_user, 5), and neither the count nor the limit is readable, so someone with 5 open tasks fills in the whole form before being refused. Suggest open_tasks and max_open_tasks on GET /me (or a sixth post_blocked_reason).
  2. 2. A task photo can’t be removed.Photos can only be added after the task exists and there is no DELETE /tasks/{task_ulid}/photos/{sort}, so a wrong photo stays public until the task is cancelled. Suggest a poster-only delete while the task is open.
  3. 3. No edit after posting.There is no PATCH /tasks/{ulid}, though spec §6 runs the keyword filter "on post and edit". A typo means cancel and repost, which costs −10 standing if applicants are waiting. Decide: edit in V1, or fix the spec wording.
  4. 4. The pin can’t be checked before posting.The barangay (set from the pin) and OUTSIDE_SERVICE_AREA are only known when the post is sent, so the review can’t name the area neighbors will see and someone outside the city learns it only at the last step. Suggest a check that returns the barangay and whether the spot is served for a lat/lng (the barangay list itself is the 0003 gap).
  5. 5. The minimum offer isn’t returned./tasks/quote returns max_points but not the minimum (limits.task_min, 50), so "50 to 1,000 pts" in the hint can go stale. Suggest min_points in the quote.
  6. 6. No notice when a held task is approved or hidden.NotificationKind has no kind for moderation, so a poster whose task was held only finds out by opening My tasks. Suggest a task notice for approved and hidden.
