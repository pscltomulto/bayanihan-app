Wallet: choices to make

Twelve numbered decisions for the Wallet tab: the balance (available, held for tasks, cashing out), the history, adding points through the payment page (a test page for now), the wait for the payment to be confirmed, cashing out to a GCash or Maya number, and the payout accounts. Each shows the real options as phone screens in the approved U0–U6 style, with a recommendation. Answer by number, or say “all recommended”. Fake Dumaguete data: Ana R. (Piapi, profile complete) adds 150 pts on Wed 25 Nov to post the gutter task; Ben S. (ID verified) cashes out on Thu 26 Nov after being paid 380 pts for it.

What is fixed

U0 tokens, balance pair, fee box, alerts, sheets, Countdown, offline banner; U1 verification screens (the “Verify your ID” fix links there); U3 “Add N pts” hand-off and safe-retry rule; U5/U6 dock, timeline and sheets. Only fields that openapi.yaml and the API code return are used.

What you decide

The 12 items below. Each has 2 or 3 options; the teal-bordered one is recommended. New API gaps are listed at the end.

The U7 flow

Three paths from the Wallet tab. Decision numbers in brackets.

See your points

  1. Wallet tab: Available, Held for tasks, Cashing out, and what you can do now from cash_in / cash_out (D1, D2, D8)
  2. History by day, 20 at a time; task rows open the task, cash-in and cash-out rows open their status page (D3)

Add points (tier 1+)

  1. Amount and GCash or Maya; from U3 the shortfall is filled in (D4)
  2. Create with an Idempotency-Key → open the payment page in a new window (D5)
  3. Back in the app: “Waiting for confirmation”, polling until paid, failed or expired; points come only from the provider’s confirmation, never from coming back (D6)
  4. Result: added, declined or cancelled, or expired; back to the U3 task if that’s where you came from (D7)

Cash out (tier 2)

  1. Gate: ID verified, a payout account, not cooling down, once a day (D8)
  2. Amount, account, the ₱15 fee and what reaches GCash, then confirm (D9)
  3. Status: requested → processing → paid; or failed → refunded (D10)
  4. Payout accounts: add, make default, remove; masked numbers only (D11)

After the payment page, before the server knows.

It works while suspended and while payments are off. A payment that lands after expiry is still credited (spec 3), so “expired” never says “you lost money”.

requested → processing → paid; or failed → refunded.

GET /wallet/cash-outs/{cashOut_ulid} returns points, fee, net_amount, channel, account_number_masked, status, failure_reason, paid_at, created_at. 12

Retries on a weak signal

A retry must never charge or pay twice.

Both money POSTs take an Idempotency-Key: the same key with the same body returns the stored cash-in or cash-out with 200 and moves nothing again; a different body with that key is 409 IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT. The app makes the key when the confirm button is first tapped and keeps it (with the body) on the phone until a definite answer; changing the amount or account makes a new key. Same rule as U3 decision 11.

Already settled (not decisions)

From the spec, the API and decisions 0002–0008.

Money format

Always “1,265 pts · ₱1,265”. The cash-out fee is in points (15 pts) and what arrives is in pesos (₱985). The GCash/Maya charge for a cash-in is pesos with centavos (₱153.75), from amount_charged_centavos.

Words

“Held for tasks” (not escrow), “Cashing out” for points on their way, “Add points” (not cash-in), “Cash out”. “Payment page” for the provider checkout. “Our team” for staff corrections.

Never a credit from the return

Coming back from the payment page shows “Waiting for confirmation”. Points appear only when GET /wallet/cash-ins/{ulid} says paid (the server’s webhook did it). The app never adds points itself.

Held for review looks like processing

Fraud holds, freezes and dormant reviews on a cash-out come back as processing; the app says “Usually a few minutes; some take longer”. At the gate, a frozen wallet is under_review: “Cash-out is paused while we check your account. Your points are safe.”

Privacy

Only masked numbers (“•••• 4567”) after adding. No legal name shown; we say “your verified name”. No other user’s wallet ever appears. Task rows show the title only.

Times

Manila time, exact: “Wed 25 Nov, 10:14 AM”. “Once a day” means a Manila calendar day.

Suspended accounts

Read-only: balance, history, cash-in and cash-out status and payout accounts are visible; adding points, cashing out, adding, defaulting and removing accounts are refused (403 ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED).

Push

Cash-in and cash-out results and “payout account added” arrive as notices (spec 5.9); tapping opens the status page or the accounts screen. Push stays opt-in from Me (0002).

API gaps (new, beyond decisions 0002–0008)

Backend work; the mockup will draw around them and mark the missing data. Still open and reused here: a readable 429 retry time (0002).

  1. 1. No fee quote before paying. The provider’s charge is known only after POST /wallet/cash-ins creates the checkout (amount_charged_centavos), so the app can’t show “You pay ₱153.75” before the commit (checklist: money never a surprise). Suggest GET /wallet/cash-ins/quote?points=&channel= returning amount_charged_centavos, or the fee rate per channel on GET /wallet.
  2. 2. No list of my cash-ins (or cash-outs). An unpaid cash-in has no history row until it is paid, and there is no list endpoint, so a pending payment can only be found again from the ulid stored on this phone; on another phone, or after clearing data, it is invisible while still counting toward the 3 open payments. Suggest GET /wallet/cash-ins?status=pending (and GET /wallet/cash-outs), or a pending_cash_ins array on GET /wallet.
  3. 3. No failure or refund times on a cash-out. GET /wallet/cash-outs/{ulid} has paid_at and created_at only. The failed screen can’t say “comes back by Fri 27 Nov, 2:45 PM” and refunded can’t say when. Suggest failed_at, refund_by (failed_at + timers.failed_payout_refund_hours) and refunded_at.
  4. 4. History rows only carry {ulid} for cash-ins and cash-outs. To write “GCash •••• 4567 · ₱985” on a row the app would need one extra call per row. Suggest embedding channel, status and, for cash-outs, account_number_masked and net_amount in the history item.
  5. 5. Failure reasons aren’t documented. Cash-in failure_reason comes from the code as declined, cancelled, expired, provider_unavailable or provider_error; cash-out failure_reason is any provider code or payout_failed. Suggest a documented enum for each with a fallback, so copy can be written per reason.
  6. 6. cash_in.blocked_reason = limit_reached has two causes. It is set both when the daily maximum is used up and when 3 payments are open, so Wallet can’t say which (only the POST’s errors.limit can). Suggest separate reasons (daily_limit, open_payments) or an open_payments count on cash_in.
  7. 7. No history filter. Only needed if D3 option B is chosen: GET /wallet/history?kind=.
  8. 8. Corrections have no reason for the user. An adjustment row shows only the amount; “Correction by our team” can’t say why. Suggest a short user-facing note on adjustment items (staff internal notes stay private).
  9. 9. No push when payments go live. D2 option B promised a notice; if the owner wants that, the API needs a notification for it. Not needed with the recommended option.
