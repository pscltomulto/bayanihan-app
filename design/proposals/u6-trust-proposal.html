Ratings, disputes and reports: choices to make

Twelve numbered decisions for what happens when a task ends well or badly: rating each other, reviews on profiles, the poster’s “Report a problem” (a dispute) with evidence, the worker’s answer, the team’s decision and how the money moves, and reporting a task, person, message or review. Each shows the real options as phone screens in the approved U0–U5 style, with a recommendation. Answer by number, or say “all recommended”. Fake Dumaguete data: Ana R. posted the gutter task in Piapi (400 pts), Ben S. did it; proof sent Thu 26 Nov, 9:40 AM.

What is fixed

U0 tokens, chips, alerts, sheets, Countdown; U2 profile and review list; U3 form fields and photo tiles; U4 ⋯ menus; U5 task page, step bar, dock, “Not quite done?” sheet, proof form and chat. Only fields in openapi.yaml and the API code are used.

The U6 flow

Two paths after the work is sent. Decision numbers in brackets.

It went fine → ratings

  1. completed (Ana confirms, auto-release, or a dispute ends in release or split): each side may rate for 14 days (D1–D2)
  2. Your rating stays hidden until the other rates or 7 days pass (D3)
  3. Visible ratings show on profiles, tagged As worker / As poster; each can be reported (D4)

It didn’t → a dispute

  1. Ana: “Report a problem” from assigned, in_progress, or proof_submitted before auto_release_at (D5–D6). Points stay held, auto-pay stops.
  2. Ben: told, 24 h to give his side; late answers still read (D7). Ben has no dispute button of his own (D12).
  3. Both: one dispute page, add photos up to 4 per side (D8)
  4. Team decides within 72 h: release, refund or split; note shown to both; read-only after (D9)

Anytime → reports

  1. Task, person, chat message, visible review (D10)
  2. Reason + optional details; nobody is told; 3 ID-verified reports hide a task from the feed (D11)

GET /tasks/{ulid} · rating, dispute→POST …/rating·GET /users/{ulid}/ratings·POST /tasks/{task_ulid}/dispute→GET /disputes/{ulid}→POST …/statement·POST …/evidence·POST /reports

API gaps (new, beyond decisions 0002–0007)

Backend work; the mockup will draw around them and mark the missing data. Still open and reused here: a readable 429 retry time (0002), tasks_done on the person block (0004).

  1. 1. No dispute outcome on the task. The dispute block on GET /tasks/{ulid} has status and due times but not outcome, worker_share or resolved_at, so the task page needs a second call to say what happened. Suggest adding those three.
  2. 2. No fee or net amounts on a decided dispute. GET /disputes/{ulid} gives worker_share only. The fee on a split (max(5%, 10), capped at the share), what the worker receives and what goes back to the poster must not be computed by the client. Suggest fee, worker_receives, poster_refund on resolved disputes.
  3. 3. No reveal time for hidden ratings. rating on the task has rate_until but not when a hidden rating becomes visible (completed_at + timers.rating_reveal_days). Suggest reveal_at.
  4. 4. No photo allowance on the dispute. The 4-per-side limit is a setting (limits.max_dispute_photos) that the client can’t read. Suggest photos_max (or photos_left for the viewer’s side).
  5. 5. No list of tasks waiting for my rating. /me/tasks and /me/jobs cards carry no rating state, so “Rate Ben” can’t show on lists or as a badge. Suggest can_rate and rate_until on those cards.
  6. 6. No “my reports”. A 200 repeat tells the app a report exists, but nothing lists them, so “You reported this” can’t persist across visits, and the reporter never learns the outcome. Owner choice: keep it silent (spec), or add GET /me/reports with a status.
  7. 7. Ratings and disputes aren’t broadcast. A new dispute, the worker’s answer and the ruling reach an open page only by refresh (Reverb sends message.sent only; 0007 gap). Suggest dispute.updated on the task channel.
  8. 8. Docs. openapi.yaml leaves out 403 ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED on rating, dispute, statement and evidence; 422 field errors for stars, reason, statement length and photo size; the dispute and rating blocks in the GET /tasks/{ulid} example; and that the worker’s report reasons differ from the dispute reasons.
