Ratings, disputes and reports

What happens when a task ends well or badly, for both sides: rating each other with hidden-until-both ratings, reviews on profiles, the poster’s “Report a problem” with evidence, the worker’s answer, the dispute page, the team’s decision with the money shown, and reporting a task, a person, a message or a review. Built from the owner’s answers on the U6 proposal (all recommended) with U0–U5 tokens and components. Every frame lists its endpoint. Fake Dumaguete data: Ana R. posted the 400 pts gutter task in Piapi, Ben S. did it; proof sent Thu 26 Nov, 9:40 AM. Nobody’s phone, legal name, exact pin or standing is shown, and no staff-only notes.

API gaps (new, beyond decisions 0002–0007)

Backend work; the mockup will draw around them and mark the missing data. Still open and reused here: a readable 429 retry time (0002), tasks_done on the person block (0004).

  1. 1. No dispute outcome on the task. The dispute block on GET /tasks/{ulid} has status and due times but not outcome, worker_share or resolved_at, so the task page needs a second call to say what happened. Suggest adding those three.
  2. 2. No fee or net amounts on a decided dispute. GET /disputes/{ulid} gives worker_share only. The fee on a split (max(5%, 10), capped at the share), what the worker receives and what goes back to the poster must not be computed by the client. Suggest fee, worker_receives, poster_refund on resolved disputes.
  3. 3. No reveal time for hidden ratings. rating on the task has rate_until but not when a hidden rating becomes visible (completed_at + timers.rating_reveal_days). Suggest reveal_at.
  4. 4. No photo allowance on the dispute. The 4-per-side limit is a setting (limits.max_dispute_photos) that the client can’t read. Suggest photos_max (or photos_left for the viewer’s side).
  5. 5. No list of tasks waiting for my rating. /me/tasks and /me/jobs cards carry no rating state, so “Rate Ben” can’t show on lists or as a badge. Suggest can_rate and rate_until on those cards.
  6. 6. No “my reports”. A 200 repeat tells the app a report exists, but nothing lists them, so “You reported this” can’t persist across visits, and the reporter never learns the outcome. The owner kept it silent for now; if wanted later, add GET /me/reports with a status.
  7. 7. Ratings and disputes aren’t broadcast. A new dispute, the worker’s answer and the ruling reach an open page only by refresh (Reverb sends message.sent only; 0007 gap). Suggest dispute.updated on the task channel.
  8. 8. Docs. openapi.yaml leaves out 403 ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED on rating, dispute, statement and evidence; 422 field errors for stars, reason, statement length and photo size; the dispute and rating blocks in the GET /tasks/{ulid} example; and that the worker’s report reasons differ from the dispute reasons.
